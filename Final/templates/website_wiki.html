Understanding Numpad Notation

To explain complex attack strings, a standard numpad notation system is used. This matches the layout of a standard keyboard numberpad, mapping directional inputs to numbers.

The buttons correspond exactly to positions relative to a joystick in neutral (5):

  • 5 is Neutral (no direction)
  • 2 is Down / Crouching
  • j. stands for a Jumping / Airborne state done by pressing 'W' on the keyboard

Examples:

  • A neutral light attack is written as 5A
  • A crouching medium attack is written as 2B
  • A jumping heavy attack is written as j.5C
